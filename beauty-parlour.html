Our Story

A studio built on care, not just cosmetics

Adaa Beauty Lounge opened its doors in 2013 with a simple belief — that beauty rituals passed down through Indian households, ubtan, oiling, potli massage, deserve a place alongside modern technique. Every service here is performed by trained stylists using dermatologist-reviewed products, in a calm, private space designed to slow you down.

From your first haircut with us to your wedding morning, we aim to be the studio you trust with the moments that matter.
